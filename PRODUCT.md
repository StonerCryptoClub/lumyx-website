# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders and owner-operators of growing online businesses (e-commerce, coaching/course creators, service businesses) doing roughly $20k–$500k/mo, plus their marketing leads. They arrive from paid ads, LinkedIn/Instagram, or referrals — skeptical, numbers-first, burned before by agencies that ran traffic and disappeared. They decide in under two minutes whether Lumyx are operators or another template agency. Primary job-to-be-done: figure out "can these people actually grow my revenue?" and book a free Growth Audit call if yes.

## Product Purpose

Lumyx Consulting (lumyx.co) is a performance-marketing agency selling one connected system: strategy, paid media, funnels, CRM/follow-up, and practical AI — "the full revenue path," not deliverables. The website's single success metric is booked Growth Audit calls (GHL booking + hero form submissions, tracked via GA4/Google Ads/Meta). Everything on the page exists to earn that booking.

## Brand Personality

Engineered, direct, revenue-obsessed. The voice of a senior operator showing you the numbers, not a creative studio showing you moodboards. Confident without hype; specific ("43% CPA drop in month one") over superlative ("world-class results"). Emotional target: the visitor should feel *"these people run tighter systems than my current agency."*

## Anti-references

- The generic dark AI-SaaS landing page: near-black + neon gradient text + glassmorphism cards + identical icon-card grids + tiny uppercase eyebrow above every section. (The A version drifted here; Phases 1–3 pulled it back. The B version must not re-enter this lane from the light side.)
- The cream/beige "AI editorial" default: warm parchment background + display serif + ruled columns. Wrong register — Lumyx is an operator brand, not a magazine.
- Agency-portfolio maximalism (WebGL noise, cursor trails, 8-second loaders) that sacrifices conversion speed for awards-site vibes. This page has a job.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** A conversion agency's own site must be a masterclass in conversion: instant clarity, one primary CTA, zero friction to booking.
2. **Numbers are the imagery.** Proof (ROAS multiples, retention rates, CPA drops) carries more brand weight than any stock photo. Give metrics typographic star treatment.
3. **Engineered, not decorated.** Every visual element earns its place in the revenue argument. Motion demonstrates precision, never fills silence.
4. **Amber is the signature.** The amber/gold accent is the brand's identity across A and B versions; it marks value, action, and proof — never wallpaper.
5. **Speed is credibility.** Fast load, instant response, no layout shift. A slow marketing agency site is a self-refutation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum: 4.5:1 body contrast, 44px touch targets on controls, 24px on links, full keyboard navigability, visible focus states. All scroll/entrance animation gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion` with crossfade fallbacks; content always visible without JS.

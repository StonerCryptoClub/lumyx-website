Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumyx Consulting ("Lumyx", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use our website, submit forms, book appointments, or communicate with us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact and lead data: name, email, phone number, company/business name, requested service, and information you submit in forms.
  • Booking data: appointment date/time and related scheduling details submitted through our booking widget provider.
  • Device and usage data: browser type, pages visited, referral source, and interaction events collected through analytics and advertising tools.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and deliver requested services.
  • Schedule and manage growth audit and strategy call appointments.
  • Send operational communications and requested marketing updates.
  • Measure site performance, improve campaigns, and optimize user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against misuse or fraud.

SMS and A2P Messaging Disclosures

If you provide a phone number and check the SMS consent box on our website form, you opt in to receive text messages (SMS/MMS) from Lumyx Consulting. This is a conversational and informational messaging program used for the following use cases: appointment reminders and confirmations, scheduling and follow-up for your free growth audit or strategy call, responses to your inquiries, and occasional service updates related to the services you requested.

  • Message frequency: message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates: message and data rates may apply, charged by your mobile carrier.
  • Opt out: you can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP. After you reply STOP, we will send a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.
  • Help: reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at Lumyxagency@gmail.com or (248) 238-2704.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • SMS consent is never a condition of purchase and is not required to submit our forms.
  • You must be at least 18 years old to opt in to receive SMS messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in consent and phone numbers collected for the purpose of SMS are not shared, sold, or rented to any third parties or affiliates under any circumstances.
